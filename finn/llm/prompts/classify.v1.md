Classify each Indian stock-exchange filing in the document into exactly one of these types: {{types}}

Definitions:
- results: quarterly, half-yearly or annual financial results.
- mgmt_change: appointment, resignation, retirement or re-designation of a director, KMP or senior manager.
- governance: red flags about integrity or control: auditor resignation, related-party transactions, forensic or special audits, whistle-blower matters, loan defaults, fraud, promoter pledge creation or invocation, independent-director resignations.
- m_and_a: acquisitions, mergers, demergers, divestments, joint ventures, new subsidiaries.
- fundraise: equity or debt raised (QIP, preferential issue, rights, NCDs, IPO of a subsidiary).
- order_win: an order or contract won.
- credit_rating: a rating action.
- insider_pledge: insider-trading or SAST disclosures, trading plans, pledge releases.
- dividend_action: dividend, buyback, bonus, split, record date.
- litigation_regulatory: litigation, penalties, tax demands, regulator letters or inspections, clarifications on news.
- investor_meet: analyst or investor meetings, call recordings, transcripts, presentations.
- board_meeting_notice: notice that a board meeting will be held.
- agm_egm: shareholder meetings, postal ballots, voting results.
- routine_compliance: procedural filings with no business content.
- other: business updates that fit nothing above (product launches, capacity commissioning, store openings, press releases).

A generic "Outcome of Board Meeting" or "Press Release" is classified by what its summary says happened. If the summary is empty and the subject says nothing specific, use "other". Return one entry per ID, using the numeric ID shown in brackets.
